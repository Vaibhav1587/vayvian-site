YouTube channel · AI video pipeline

The Royale Lab

A Clash Royale Shorts channel where the creator's job is reduced to two things: play the game and press upload. Everything in between is an automated pipeline I built in Python.

  • Computer vision reads the game HUD to detect overtime, tower falls and match-deciding moments.
  • A ranking engine scores every replay on emotion, surprise, clarity, story and skill, and drops the boring ones.
  • Hook-first editing with FFmpeg: the climax leads, a targeted zoom aims at the falling tower, subtitles and thumbnail are generated.
  • A local LLM (Ollama) writes titles, descriptions and hashtags. It never touches a frame.
  • Fully offline, zero recurring cost, a few hundred automated tests keep it honest.

Health app · Flutter + FastAPI

Yoga Companion

A personalized, safety-first yoga assistant. Instead of playing videos, it builds a daily plan from your goals, experience and health conditions, and tells you clearly which poses to avoid and why.

  • A deterministic, explainable rule engine. Tell it about hypertension or back pain and unsafe poses are removed before the plan is built.
  • Every recommendation comes with a reason and step-by-step guidance. No black box.
  • The whole app, pose library and explanations in English, हिन्दी and मराठी.
  • Consistent pose illustrations rendered on my own GPU with Stable Diffusion, a LoRA per character and ControlNet skeletons.
  • Flutter front end, FastAPI + PostgreSQL back end, one docker compose up from a fresh clone.

Kids' game · Godot 4 · Android

Little Shop: Math Adventure

A cheerful shopkeeping game for children aged 6 to 10. Serve customers, add up prices, take payments and count out the change. Every sale earns coins to decorate your own little shop.

  • 30 hand-crafted levels from counting to 5 all the way to multiplication and giving change.
  • No fail states. Mistakes get a gentle retry from Biscuit the helper, never a buzzer.
  • No ads, no purchases, no accounts and zero Android permissions. Nothing ever leaves the phone.
  • Plays in English, हिन्दी and मराठी. Saves survive corrupt files and mid-write kills, with a disk-level test for every failure mode.
