Privacy Policy

Little Shop: Math Adventure (com.vayvian.mathadventure) — by VAYVIAN TECHNOLOGIES

Effective date: 27 August 2026

The short version: this app collects no data. None.
No personal information, no identifiers, no analytics, no ads, no accounts — and it never connects to the internet.

1. Who we are

Little Shop: Math Adventure is a children's educational game made by VAYVIAN TECHNOLOGIES. It teaches mathematics (counting, addition, money and change) to children aged 6–10 through shopkeeping play.

2. Information we collect

We collect no information of any kind. The app requests zero Android permissions — you can verify this on the app's Play Store listing or in your device's app settings. It has no access to the internet, contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or any device identifier.

3. Data stored on the device

Game progress (completed levels, stars, coins, decorations, and sound settings) is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. It never leaves the device, is not visible to other apps, and is permanently deleted when the app is uninstalled. No backup of this data is made to any server.

4. Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and is built to the strictest reading of children's privacy laws (including COPPA and GDPR): since no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no social features, no chat, no links out of the app, and no content a child can share or receive.

5. Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and makes no network connections — so no data is shared with anyone, because none exists to share.

6. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above (for example, an optional cloud backup), this policy will be updated before that version is released, with the effective date above revised. The current version of this policy always applies to the current version of the app.
